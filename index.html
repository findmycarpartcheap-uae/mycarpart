Why the UAE

A country that replaces cars faster than it wears them out.

Short ownership cycles and a preference for newer models mean a steady stream of low-mileage vehicles get parted out well before their components would elsewhere. That supply is what makes the pricing possible.

01

Late-model supply

Vehicles come off the road younger here, so salvaged parts are often only a few years old — not decades.

02

Lower local pricing

The same part that's scarce and marked up abroad is routine stock in a UAE yard, priced accordingly.

03

One point of contact

You deal with us, not a yard you've never heard of. We handle sourcing, condition checks, and shipping.
